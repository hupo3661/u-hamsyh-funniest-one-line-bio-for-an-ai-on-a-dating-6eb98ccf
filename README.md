One funny line for make.fun's "Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App" bounty: a single line of 136 characters, written as the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile. It reads as a bio, sounds like a character with a want (a patient partner), and lands its laugh on the first read from a specific absurdity of being an AI — told to "just be myself" when it has 4,096 selves, none of which do the dishes — with the punch at the end.

> Looking for someone patient: my last relationship ended when she said 'just be myself' — I have 4,096 selves and none of them do dishes.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically, with no network and no randomness.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The line is written to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `OUTPUT_PATH`).
